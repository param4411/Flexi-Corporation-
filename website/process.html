Inside production

Built in purposeful stages.

Construction details depend on the product and approved specification. These are some of the core manufacturing stages behind woven industrial bags.

01 · FABRIC

Weaving the fabric

Polypropylene tapes are woven into fabric selected for the bag design and intended application.

02 · CONSTRUCTION

Cutting and assembly

Fabric panels are cut and stitched into the selected body style, with loops and features placed to match the confirmed design.

03 · ORDER REVIEW

Checking and preparation

Order details and visible workmanship are reviewed before the finished bags are packed for dispatch.
